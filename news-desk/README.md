# News Desk

**Its job:** make our own content from the sourced pool and onboard creators.

**What it produces:** text, images and video.

## What lives here

- **Content formats:** what each kind of our own content looks like, for the launch categories (PLAN.md step 1.3).
- **Our own content (the made route):** made from the sourced pool, the news and creators' takes our bots find on X, and crediting the found posts it builds on. The tools that prepare it and load it into the shared content store live here; images and video files go into the content store's storage, not into this repository.
- **Creators:** the list of creators to invite, and how they are onboarded through creator accounts in the app, where they post directly (the posted route; steps 1.3 and 4.4).

## Rules

- What passes to Product is defined by the hand-over agreement, [agreement/README.md](../agreement/README.md): a creation (text, one image or one video) with its route, and the shared lists of locations and categories. News Desk reads sourced items, in the agreement's shape, from the sourced pool. No content is written before its first half is agreed (step 0.7).
- Our own content never copies a found post's words, pictures or video; it links and credits them.
- Projects never copy code between each other; this shared repository is the only route.
- Frameworks and dependencies aren't chosen yet.
