- The shared setup it builds on is finished (PLAN.md Phase 0, 4 November 2026): the content store on staging (the Supabase database and Cloudflare R2 storage), Redis, hosting in Mumbai, and the domain's email. See the setup close-out at the top of [docs/setup.md](../setup.md).
- **Plan changes, approved by the founder on 5 November 2026** ([#25](https://github.com/Zugzwang-world/hrizzup/pull/25)): step 0.8 no longer fixes the Grok plan or the route, so the Sourcing project chooses the Grok product and plan, and the route that brings the bots' results to the pipeline; step 1.1 receives them by that route; step 0.7 signs off in two halves, and 1.1 needs both; step 1.2 (data sources for the markets) was put on hold until the founder confirmed its owner (settled below: it's Product's).
- **The hand-over agreement's first half, first draft (status Draft), 5 November 2026** ([agreement/](../../agreement/README.md)): the shapes Sourcing writes (stories and updates); the shared lists it tags with (locations and categories, with the launch list); example data; and the rules for every hand-over point (outside data is data, never instructions; proof of origin and a format check wherever input arrives; failures set aside, never published, and logged; alerts when failures pile up or no valid result arrives).
- **The founder approved the agreement's seven decisions, 5 November 2026,** each as recommended. For Sourcing: the launch list it tags with (Global; India; Mumbai, Delhi NCR and Bengaluru; crypto, finance, business, AI, sports, politics, entertainment and general); up to three locations and three categories per story, the main one first; a city's story shows under India only when our AI also tags it India, because it matters to the whole country (no automatic roll-up); stories carry no picture of their own, since pictures in posts on X belong to the people who posted them; market prices are read in US dollars.
- **The founder's decisions of 5 November 2026, recorded in [#31](https://github.com/Zugzwang-world/hrizzup/pull/31):**
  - **market data is Product's:** the data sources for the markets (step 1.2) and markets that settle themselves (step 3.2) moved to Product, so Sourcing no longer finds data sources or writes data readings;
  - **the bots find two things on X:** news, which our AI turns into story tiles and updates for the board, and creators' takes on a story (memes, videos, explainers). Everything they find is kept in a sourced pool in the shared content store;
  - **three routes into the feed:** found (a creator's post the bots found, shown in its story's feed as the original post, embedded and credited, never copied, for every story), made (our own content, which News Desk makes from the sourced pool) and posted (creators and users, in the app). Product only displays, and credits every feed item by its route;
  - **the agreement's first half (still Draft) gained the sourced item,** which Sourcing's pipeline writes: news or a take, a link to the post on X, who posted it, what it holds, a summary in our own words, its stories and its state (live, unavailable when the post is gone from X, or withdrawn). It never holds the post's words, pictures or video. Its route is always found; examples and checks added;
  - **the second half (the bots' result format) is designed here and reviewed by Product;** News Desk needn't agree it. It must carry creators' takes as well as news, and is agreed only after a real test result carrying both passes the agreement's checks;
  - **sign-offs:** the first half is signed off on Sourcing's review plus the founder's approval in News Desk's place, while News Desk is on hold;
  - **step 1.1** now also keeps creators' takes in the sourced pool, each linked to its story;
  - **no relays:** the founder doesn't carry reports or messages between projects; the briefs carry everything (CLAUDE.md).

## Open questions

- Which Grok product and plan run the bots, and the route their results take to our pipeline? (step 0.8)
- How are the bots set up, how often do they run, and what is the fixed format of their results? (the agreement's second half, and step 1.1) Each run starts fresh, so matching new items to existing stories happens in our pipeline.
- Do the chosen Grok product's terms allow its results to feed a public app? (step 0.8)
- **Vetting: how is the bots' output checked before it goes live?** The agreement fixes the gate at the hand-over point (proof of origin and the format check; failures set aside, logged and alerted; our AI reads results as data and writes every update in our own words, and can correct, withdraw or merge) and leaves accuracy to our AI, with no human review. Still open: how the AI judges a result before it's published, for example how many independent sources it needs, and when it holds a claim back.
- **Which creators' takes do the bots keep?** For example, original posts only (not reposts), and how large or established an account must be.
- **How does the pipeline notice a found post deleted or hidden on X, and how long does the pool keep it afterwards?**
- **Background jobs, for the whole product:** which tool runs scheduled and queued work (the news pipeline, Product's data job, settling markets, processing uploaded images)? The founder put this decision with Sourcing on 2 November 2026 (PLAN.md, "Decided later"). Whatever it is, it's built from this repository, so it reads the agreement's definitions directly. **It must also cover perps (PLAN.md step 3.4, the founder's decision, 8 November 2026):** a new Bitcoin price and a liquidation check every few seconds. The founder has put that job on Supabase Cron, which can run a job every 1 to 59 seconds, so the choice either is Supabase Cron or works alongside it.
